Portfolio project 08

Word guess

A five-letter word guessing game: you have six attempts. After each guess the tiles show which letters are in the right place, in the word but elsewhere, or not in the word. A small learning project built with AI assistance, not affiliated with any other game.

Play

  • ✓ Right letter, right place
  • ~ In the word, wrong place
  • × Not in the word

Type with your keyboard or tap the keys. Enter sends the guess, Backspace deletes. Words come from a small built-in list, so a few real words may be rejected. The word of the day is picked from the date, no server involved.
